# C01 · Capstone Declaration

**Meetings:** D85–D86 · **Points:** 15 pts

**Video/Source Link(s):** None. Read the capstone spec in the syllabus before you fill this out.

**Notes**  
Declare three things: format (solo or pair, maximum two students, no exceptions), tier (1, 2, or 3), and if pairing, partner and ownership split.

Tier sets a grade ceiling, not a floor. A sloppy Tier 3 scores below an immaculate Tier 1. Do not pick a tier to impress me. Pick the one you can finish and defend.

Tier 1 is an honorable choice. It is not the remedial option. It is for someone who would rather go deep on execution quality than wide on scope.

Pairs are cross-track by default. A same-track pair needs a written justification for why the project needs two people from the same side. Usually it doesn't.

Tier 3 requires my pre-approval and an external reviewer identified by the skeleton gate on Thu Apr 22. Come talk to me during class this week if you're considering it. Do not declare Tier 3 without that conversation.

**Deliverable**  
`CAPSTONE_DECLARATION.md`: format, tier, partner (if any), one paragraph on what you intend to build and why it clears the floor.

**Reflection Questions**

1. Which format did you pick, and what is the honest reason, not the reason that sounds good?
2. Which tier, and what specifically about that tier's bar do you think you can hit?
3. Name the part of your idea you are least sure you can build. What would you do if it turns out to be impossible in week 3 of 6?
4. What does your project do that "we built a chatbot" doesn't? Point at the specific floor requirement it clears.
5. If pairing: what does your partner bring that you cannot do yourself? If solo: what are you giving up by not pairing?
6. What is your success metric: the number that will tell you whether this worked? Write it now, before you build anything.
7. What is your baseline for that metric, and how will you measure it?
8. What existing work of yours (your v2 agent, your Week 22 GPT, your RAG pipeline) does this build on, and what is new?
