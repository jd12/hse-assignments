# C03 · Sprint 1

**Meetings:** D89–D91 · **Points:** 15 pts

**Video/Source Link(s):** None.

**Notes**  
Standups start this week. Ten minutes per project, delivered to the whole class, not privately to me. You will present three things: what you did, what you're doing next, what's blocking you.

Commit early and often. A sprint with one giant commit on Friday is a sprint I cannot help you with on Wednesday.

Get the ugly end-to-end path working before you make any part of it good. A pipeline that runs badly beats three excellent components that have never been connected.

**Deliverable**  
Commit log for the week + standup delivered + `SPRINT1.md` (one paragraph: what works, what doesn't, what changed about the plan).

**Reflection Questions**

1. What works end to end right now, however badly?
2. What did you discover this week that your proposal got wrong?
3. What is blocking you, and what have you already tried?
4. Did your success metric survive contact with the real data? If not, what does it need to become?
5. How many commits did you make this week, and what does the distribution across days tell you about how you work?
