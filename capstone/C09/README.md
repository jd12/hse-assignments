# C09 · Public Symposium (FINAL)

**Meetings:** D106 · **Points:** 15 pts

**Video/Source Link(s):** None.

**Notes**  
Evening demo night. Families, faculty, and invited engineers. Eight-minute demo plus four minutes of questions. Solos and pairs, same format, same stage.

Pairs: each partner defends separately, on the whole system, not on their half. If you cannot explain the part your partner built, that is a fact about both of you: plan accordingly, and spend this week making sure it isn't true.

Any part of the system is fair game. That includes the parts that don't work.

Lead with the demo, not the architecture. Show the thing doing the thing, then explain how. An audience that has seen it work will follow the explanation; an audience that hasn't will not.

Say your number out loud. "It resolves 71% of queries correctly, up from 43%" is the sentence that separates this from a science fair.

**Deliverable**  
Symposium demo + live defense + final repository with everything committed.

**Reflection Questions**

1. What worked in the demo and what didn't?
2. What question did you get that you couldn't answer? What is the answer, now that you've had time?
3. What would you do differently with another four weeks?
4. What is the single thing you learned this year that you would not have learned by building an agent on your own?
5. Which assignment from Weeks 1–26 turned out to matter most for your capstone, and did you expect that at the time?
6. What are you going to build next?
